A Dell Inspiron 5567 boots Linux Mint today. Next year it may stop.

<!-- thesis -->
Texas law asks a small city for two things: trained employees and prompt reports of attacks.

The clerk's computer failed the State's test. It ran Windows 10 from 2015 on.

The restore proved the copy. The engineer ran one command:

```
restic restore latest
```

restic printed "Restored 302 / 301 files/dirs" in 0:00. The files came back.

The Inspiron 5567 shipped in 2016. Its maker stopped updates in 2021.
